Privacy Policy

Sokker Pro for iOS · Last updated 17 September 2026

Sokker Pro has no servers and collects no data. It is a client that talks to sokker.org on your behalf. Everything it stores stays on your iPhone.

Sokker Pro is an unofficial, independently developed companion app for the online football manager game at sokker.org. It is not produced, endorsed by, or affiliated with the operators of sokker.org. You need an existing Sokker account to use it.

1. What we collect

Nothing. The developer operates no servers, no backend and no database. The app contains no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no third-party tracking software of any kind. No information about you or your device is ever transmitted to the developer.

2. What the app stores on your device

The app needs to hold some information locally in order to work. This data never leaves your iPhone except where noted.

Your Sokker password

Your password is typed either into the app's sign-in form or directly into sokker.org's own page shown in the in-app browser. In both cases it is sent over an encrypted HTTPS connection to sokker.org only. The app does not save your password, does not write it to any log, and does not transmit it anywhere else. It is discarded from memory once sign-in completes.

Session cookies

After you sign in, sokker.org issues session cookies. These are stored on your device — in the app's own cookie store and, when the in-app browser is used, in that browser's store. They are sent only back to sokker.org, to keep you signed in. Cookies are never read by the developer or shared with anyone.

Your Sokker game data

To let you browse your squad, juniors, training and news without refetching constantly, the app caches the data it retrieves from sokker.org — including your Sokker username — in your device's local app storage. This cache is private to the app and is removed when you delete the app.

Files you export

The app can export your cached data to a JSON file. You choose where that file is saved. The app never uploads exported files anywhere.

3. Third parties

  • sokker.org — the service this app is a client for. Everything you do through the app happens on your Sokker account. How sokker.org handles your information is governed by their own terms and privacy policy, not this one.
  • Cloudflare — sokker.org sits behind Cloudflare's network. When Cloudflare asks you to complete a human-verification check, that check is handled by Cloudflare as part of sokker.org's infrastructure. The developer has no access to it.
  • Apple — distributes the app and may provide the developer with anonymous aggregate statistics (such as download counts) that cannot identify you.

There are no other third parties. No data broker, advertiser or analytics provider receives anything.

4. Your choices

  • Sign out — clears your session cookies from both the app's cookie store and the in-app browser, ending the session on this device.
  • Delete everything — delete the app from your iPhone. That removes all cached game data, cookies and settings. Nothing is retained anywhere else, because there is nowhere else.
  • Your Sokker account itself — created and deleted on sokker.org. The app cannot create or delete Sokker accounts.

5. Children

Sokker Pro is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from them.
